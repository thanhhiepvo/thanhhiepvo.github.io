Work building on the GaitGL model, which identifies people from the way they walk rather
than from facial features. The architecture pairs a global branch that captures
whole-silhouette motion with local branches over horizontal body regions, combined through
local temporal aggregation across the gait sequence — the global–local split is what lets
it pick up both overall gait rhythm and limb-level detail.
