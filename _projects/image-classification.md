Implementations of several classification backbones written from scratch in PyTorch, to
work through the design decisions behind each one — residual connections in ResNet,
multi-branch convolutions in Inception, the fire modules in SqueezeNet, and patch
embedding with self-attention in the Vision Transformer.

**Transfer learning.** Alongside the from-scratch work, EfficientNet-B0 was fine-tuned as
a pretrained baseline for comparison against the hand-built models.
