A UNet implementation for semantic segmentation, trained and evaluated on the Pascal VOC
2012 dataset. The encoder–decoder structure with skip connections recovers the spatial
detail that repeated downsampling would otherwise discard, which is what makes per-pixel
labelling viable at the output resolution.
