A comparison of one-stage and two-stage detectors on a Vietnamese traffic-sign dataset.
Traffic signs are a demanding target for detection: the objects are small relative to the
frame, and several classes differ only in their pictogram.

**Models evaluated.** Faster R-CNN and FCOS (two-stage and anchor-free), alongside
YOLOv12, RetinaNet, and SSD300 as single-shot baselines.
