Bio

I hold a B.S. in Information Technology (Computer Science) from VNUHCM – University of Science, completed in April 2026. My undergraduate thesis, on ontology-based constraints for large language models, was advised by Dr. Tien-Len Bui.

I am broadly interested in large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and computer vision. My recent work spans ontology-guided prompt engineering, parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT), and building production AI systems with vector and graph databases.

I am currently an Agentic AI Resident at VinUniversity. Previously I interned as an AI Engineer at FPT Software and spent 2025 at Queen's University Belfast, first as a Research Assistant and then as a Visiting Research Student. I am always open to collaborations and discussions. Feel free to reach out via email!
