Bio

I hold a B.S. in Information Technology (Computer Science) from VNUHCM – University of Science, completed in April 2026. My undergraduate thesis was on ontology-based constraints for large language models.

I am interested in Deep Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), Computer Vision, and Agentic AI systems.

I am currently a Machine Learning & Data Mining Engineer at VinSmart Future and an Agentic AI Resident at VinUniversity, both in Hanoi. Previously I interned as an AI Engineer at FPT Software and spent 2025 at Queen's University Belfast, first as a Research Assistant and then as a Visiting Research Student. I am always open to collaborations and discussions. Feel free to reach out via email!
